This guide explains how to create effective video scripts for TikTok and social media using the Promotional Presentations feature in Ungu Viyafaari.

**6. Sales Management**
- Sell your batches to shops
- Track payment status (pending, partial, paid)
- Automatically reduce batch inventory
- Calculate total revenue and pending payments

**7. Collections**
- Track payments received from shops
- Automatically update sale payment status
- Generate MTD/YTD collection reports

## Script Structure

### Basic Format
```
[Scene 1]
Hook: "Your attention-grabbing opening line"
Action: Describe what happens on screen
Text overlay: "Text that appears"

[Scene 2]
Hook: "Next engaging line"
Action: Next action
Text overlay: "Next text"

[Call to Action]
"Your CTA text"
"Follow for more!"
```

## Script Best Practices

### The Hook (First 3 Seconds)
- Start with something that grabs attention immediately
- Use questions, surprising statements, or visual hooks
- Examples:
  - "Wait until you see this!"
  - "This changes everything!"
  - "You don't want to miss this!"

### Scene Duration
- Keep each scene under 10 seconds
- TikTok viewers have short attention spans
- Fast-paced content performs better

### Visual Actions
- Describe exactly what happens on screen
- Include camera movements, product reveals, or demonstrations
- Make actions clear and engaging

### Text Overlays
- Keep text short and readable
- Use large, bold fonts
- Position text where it doesn't cover important visuals

### Call to Action (CTA)
- Always end with a clear CTA
- Direct viewers to take specific action
- Examples:
  - "Link in bio to order now!"
  - "Save this for later!"
  - "Follow for more recipes!"

## Tips for Success

### Audio
- Use trending sounds from TikTok's library
- Match music tempo to scene transitions
- Consider voiceovers for complex explanations

### Hashtags
- Research trending hashtags in your niche
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags per video
- Include branded hashtags for your business

### Posting Schedule
- Post when your audience is most active
- Consistency is key - aim for daily posting
- Analyze performance metrics to optimize timing

### Engagement
- Respond to comments quickly
- Encourage user-generated content
- Create duet/stitch opportunities

## Troubleshooting

### Low Engagement
- Review your hook - is it attention-grabbing?
- Check if scenes are too long
- Ensure CTAs are clear and compelling

### Poor Completion Rates
- Front-load your best content
- Maintain energy throughout
- Use pattern interrupts to retain attention

### Low Conversion
- Strengthen your CTA
- Make next steps obvious
- Create urgency or scarcity
